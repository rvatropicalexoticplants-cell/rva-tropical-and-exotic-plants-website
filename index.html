why this exists

You shouldn't have to love plants alone.

Plant care advice online is endless and contradictory, and most "communities" are just an algorithm deciding what you see next. We wanted something realer than that: a folding table, a cutting handed to you in a Solo cup, someone who'll tell you straight whether your fiddle leaf is actually dying or just dramatic.

Every swap, market, and workshop we've run since June 2021 has had the same job: put you in a room with people who'll stop what they're doing to help you save a plant. That's it. That's the whole strategy. Here's how it started.
