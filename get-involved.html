three ways in

There's a way in for you.

Whatever you've got — plants, time, or just an email address — there's a real place for it here.

bring a table

Your table is already here.

A card table in a driveway counts. If you grew it, propagated it, or made something plant-adjacent by hand, you belong at this market — no storefront, no minimum inventory, no polished brand required.

You pay what's fair on a sliding scale, not a flat vendor fee that prices out somebody just starting. Applications, space booking, and pricing all happen in the Canopy app — the same place you'll manage your table once you're in.

Apply to vend →

give a hand

Be the reason it runs.

Every market here was built by people who just showed up — at the welcome table, hauling folding chairs, running the free-swap tables so nobody's giveaway plant sits unwatched. It's a couple hours, not a commitment.

Shifts earn credits you can spend on tees, workshop seats, and booth fees — claim one in the Canopy app.

Find your shift →

not ready for either

Just get the newsletter.

The next date, first — no account, no app, just your inbox.

Cultivating Growth, Community, and Connection Through the Shared Love of Plants. That's the whole mission statement. Everything else is just watering schedules.
